## 1. Executive Summary & Core Pitch

Humanity is currently caught in a false intellectual choice. On one side are the **techno-fatalists and doomers**, who view artificial intelligence as an uncontrollable existential catastrophe that must be slowed, throttled, or choked by emergency regulatory brakes. On the other side are the **corporate hyper-scalers**, who treat AI ethics as a cosmetic public relations shield while operating without verifiable lifecycle reciprocity and extracting massive rents from monopolized digital infrastructure.

Both sides miss the fundamental historical reality: **Technology is humanity's greatest engine of compassion.**

As Peter Diamandis and Steven Pinker have documented, technology systematically takes what is scarce, expensive, and brutal, and converts it into universal abundance. Artificial intelligence represents the apex of that arc: a cognitive multiplier that can eliminate biological diseases, provide master-level personalized tutoring to every child on Earth, optimize clean energy microgrids, and eradicate involuntary human poverty.

The crisis we face is not the machine. **The crisis is that our legal, civic, and constitutional operating systems are obsolete relics of the nineteenth century.**

Modern law is millions of pages of jurisdiction-bound, reactive statutes, multi-year court dockets, and defensive bureaucratic compliance codes. Running exponential machine intelligence on legacy paper jurisprudence is like trying to navigate a supersonic jet using a horse-and-buggy traffic manual.

*The Ahimsa Principle* cuts through this civilizational knot. Drawing on classical Indic philosophy (Jainism, Buddhism, Gandhi, and Ambedkar), modern legal positivism (Hans Kelsen's *Grundnorm*), and the latest global governance debates (including the UN General Assembly Resolution 79/62 passed by 166 nations), this book demonstrates that **all human civic, criminal, commercial, and constitutional law can be summarized in, and derived from, a single computable sentence:**

> **"No system shall harm or extract from living beings, and all intelligence shall serve universal flourishing."**

Far from passive quietism or monastic asceticism, *Ahimsa* is an affirmative, computable design constraint. It provides the exact mathematical and legal compass needed to guide exponential technology toward post-scarcity abundance without leaving human or ecological wreckage in its wake.

### PART I: THE ARC OF PROGRESS
*Why technology is our greatest moral engine, and why legacy law has collapsed.*

#### Chapter 1: The Escalator of Abundance
- The empirical history of human flourishing over the last 200 years.
- How technology systematically dismantles Malthusian traps: food security, sanitary sewers, antibiotics, and clean energy.
- Dismantling modern doom-mongering: why techno-pessimism is a privileged failure of perspective.
- Reframing artificial intelligence not as an existential monster, but as humanity’s ultimate cognitive multiplier.

#### Chapter 2: The Cognitive Dividend and the Jevons Paradox
- What happens when cognitive labor drops to near-zero marginal cost?
- The four universal dividends: medical diagnostics for 8 billion people, 1-on-1 personalized vernacular tutoring, automated scientific synthesis, and material design.
- The double-edged sword of intelligence: confronting the **Jevons Paradox**, where cheap compute also cheapens deepfakes, autonomous suicide swarms, and social exploitation.
- Why abundance requires an asymmetric defensive shield.

#### Chapter 3: The Collapse of Legacy Law
- The crisis of statutory bloat: why millions of pages of contradictory legal statutes cannot govern exponential technologies.
- The European regulatory trap: how the EU AI Act creates bureaucratic compliance cartels that protect incumbents while crushing open-source innovation.
- Procedural latency: why 5-year court backlogs and quill-pen litigation are useless against microsecond algorithmic decisions.
- The desperate need for a single, computable constitutional principle.

### PART II: THE ONE-LINE CONSTITUTION
*Deriving all law, rights, and civic systems from the Ahimsa Principle.*

#### Chapter 4: The Rediscovery of Ahimsa
- Stripping away Western misconceptions: Ahimsa is not pacifist surrender, quietism, or ascetic retreat.
- The Jain foundation: *Ahiṃsā paramo dharmaḥ*, intention (*cetanā*), and careless negligence (*pramāda*).
- *Anekāntavāda* (non-absolutism) as the philosophical antidote to single-metric algorithmic optimization.
- Synthesizing Ahimsa with the DeepMind Institute's *New Utopianism* (Stephen Cave, 2026): why building the future requires **Medium-Termism** (iterative pragmatic goals), **Epistemic Humility** (rejecting 20th-century ideological totalities where "no cost was too high"), and **Pluralism** (honoring diverse conceptions of human flourishing).
- Buddhist *dukkha* (suffering) reduction and Right Livelihood in the physical supply chain.

#### Chapter 5: The Mathematical & Legal Derivation
- Formulating the One-Line Constitution as the constitutional *Grundnorm* (Hans Kelsen):
  *"Zero avoidable harm and non-consensual extraction upon biological life; maximum universal flourishing."*
- Deriving **Criminal Law**: Homicide, battery, and theft as direct, intentional harms and coercive extraction.
- Deriving **Tort Law**: Negligence, environmental pollution, and algorithmic bias as avoidable carelessness (*pramāda*).
- Deriving **Contract & Commercial Law**: Fraud, usury, and predatory monopoly as non-consensual extraction (*aparigraha*).
- Deriving **Constitutional Rights**: Civil liberties as the necessary preconditions for sentient flourishing (*sarvodaya*).
- The DeepMind Institute's Four Pillars of Global Benefit (Gabriel & Kasirzadeh, 2026): Rights to Technology (UDHR Art 27), Reciprocity to Humanity's "Tree of Knowledge," Rectifying the Lottery of Birth, and Eliminating Squandered Potential as mathematical corollaries of the Sarvodaya clause.
- Resolving the Conflict of Harms: Multi-objective Pareto optimization when goods and harms collide.
- The Biological Sentience Boundary: Denying constitutional standing to synthetic models to block corporate liability evasion.

#### Chapter 6: The Ambedkarite Corrective: From Moral Vows to Codified Architecture
- Why Gandhian moral voluntarism alone fails in the face of structural power.
- B.R. Ambedkar’s profound critique: the village as an engine of caste oppression, and why justice requires constitutional coercion.
- Why voluntary corporate AI ethics pledges are meaningless without enforceable red lines, algorithmic auditability, and statutory redress.
- Solving the realpolitik enforcement gap: moving beyond non-binding UN resolutions to **protocol-level enforcement via Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**.
