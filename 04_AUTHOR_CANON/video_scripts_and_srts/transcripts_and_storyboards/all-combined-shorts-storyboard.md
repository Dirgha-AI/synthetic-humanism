## Counts (measured, not guessed)
- Whisper text is lowercase with almost no periods → **~5 punctuated "sentences"**. Meaningless unit.
- Real units: **90 spoken beats, 1,546 words, 632.4s**, mean beat 7.03s.

## Cadence rules
1. B-roll changes every **2.5–4s**. Never hold one visual >6s (exceptions: sindoor macro in
   Act I, final Earthrise hold in Close).
2. Pattern interrupt every **20–30s**: scale jump (macro↔aerial), B&W↔color, 1s silence +
   title card, or speed ramp. Mark every ~4th beat with ★ in your edit.
3. Face rule: confession beats (Acts V–VII) = full masked talking head (`only talking head.png`);
   exposition beats = cutout small-right + B-roll-dominant left.
4. Text on screen: one keyword per beat max (burn from SRT), never full sentences.
