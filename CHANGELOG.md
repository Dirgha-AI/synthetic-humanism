# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-18)

First public release.

- Manifesto, essays, TEDx talk transcript and video subtitles.
- Stage 1 scorer and manifest compiler (AIA v1.1), JSON Schema, 50-system benchmark with a 1,000-trial sensitivity check, and 13 unit tests.
- Twenty documented AI failures rebuilt as test cases for both stages: 10 refused at Stage 1, 10 stopped at Stage 2.
- Fiscal simulator for non-labour revenue and Universal Basic Capital.
- A copy of LOKAHI v0.1.3. The benchmark binds the votes it collects to each action, as v0.1.3 requires.
